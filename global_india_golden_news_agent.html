How This In-Browser AI News Agent Works

A single-file, zero-install HTML app combining live GDELT DOC 2.0 Global News discovery with an embedded Gemini AI Verification & Golden News Agent.

1

Dynamic Query Builder

Converts your category + timeframe (timespan or custom dates) into live GDELT DOC 2.0 API queries.

2

India + Global Discovery

Fetches live Indian (sourcecountry:IN) and worldwide newspaper coverage directly in JSON with RSS fallback.

3

AI Verification Agent

Gemini cross-checks stories across independent newspapers, flags clickbait/fluff, and verifies credibility.

4

Golden News Synthesis

Extracts each newspaper's exact focus, assigns a 1–10 signal score, and highlights only to-the-point Golden News.

5

Sheets & Report Export

Exports 1-click Google Sheets TSV copy, CSV download, and standalone printable HTML briefings.
